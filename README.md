# Unified Agenda Lateness Tracker

A public counter tracking how late the U.S. federal Unified Agenda is.

**Live:** https://unified-agenda-tracker.abulix.workers.dev/

## What is this?

The federal government is legally required to publish the Unified Agenda 
of regulatory actions twice a year, under Executive Order 12866 and the
Regulatory Flexibility Act (5 U.S.C. § 602). The Unified Agenda is the
public's main window into what regulations agencies are working on.

The Fall 2025 edition is overdue. This page counts the days.

## What's here today

A static page with a live-computed counter. No backend yet. The number
updates daily based on the visitor's date.
