Regulatory Transparency Tracker

The Fall 2025 Unified Agenda is 130 days overdue.

The federal government is legally required to publish the Unified Agenda of regulatory actions twice a year. The Fall 2025 edition was due by December 31, 2025. It has not been published.

Why this matters

The Unified Agenda is the public's main window into what regulations federal agencies are working on. Without it, the public, the press, and regulated industries are left guessing about which rules are coming, which have been quietly shelved, and which are years behind schedule.

Publication is required by Executive Order 12866 and the Regulatory Flexibility Act (5 U.S.C. § 602). Both require semiannual publication.

The pattern is broader than one missing edition. The Spring 2025 edition was itself published on September 4, 2025 — three to five months past its historical window. Late publication has become routine.

Each bar shows how many days passed between consecutive Unified Agenda editions. The legally implied cadence is twice a year — roughly 180 days. The final red bar is the time elapsed since the most recent edition (Spring 2025, published September 22, 2025), and it's still growing.
